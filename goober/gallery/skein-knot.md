# Skein knot shelf

Seven owner-designated keeper pictures live in the Goober PWA Atlas: Caschae’s
porchLight and princess letter send-off, four BAMBI story-work pictures, and the
skeinIT work-table plate.

For this public projection, each preserved WebP is both source and display. Its
filename, dimensions, byte length, and SHA-256 are recorded in `catalog.json`;
no missing larger original is implied. These pictures are story evidence, not
device authority or proof that depicted software exists.

The Tower thread reuses the already-public `peachfall-09` concept rather than
duplicating it. Player choices remain device-local candidates until explicitly
kept in the existing Goober field log.
