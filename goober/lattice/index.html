The public address is where the app lives today. These names describe what you are opening, so a later local home can serve the same resource without inventing another world.

Local home and private media are not connected yet.

No browser scan runs when you open this page. An address, family relationship, QR code or gallery click does not grant access to a home computer or its files.

What these names do—and do not do

These are resource identifiers understood by this page’s small resolver. They are not installed operating-system links or live local DNS records. Opening a public route is not an instruction to an agent.

A future home-media path needs an exact selected collection, authenticated access, expiry and revocation, and successful allowed/denied tests. Until then this page exposes no private share and forwards no credentials.
