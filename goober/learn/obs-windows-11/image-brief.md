# OBS first recording / image communications

Six-card visual series for an independent Saelion field guide. Concept: software is not a mystery box; keep the moment and know where it goes. Use spacious dark plum, lantern gold and cool cyan, an abstract recording monitor, three distinct folder forms, and a quiet waveform. No fake Windows dialog or pretend installation receipt. Existing fantasy landscape is backdrop only, not the subject of the instructions.

Each 1200×675 card has one clear action and one proof. Text remains deterministic and legible. QR payload is the exact published step URL; final rendered PNG must be decoded before marking it verified. Keep a readable guide URL because metadata may be stripped. No credentials, usernames or real desktop screenshots. Preserve the source card text and artifact hashes.

For later generative art: create an editorial landscape illustration of a warm personal recording workspace, lavender/cyan rim lighting, three floating folders representing app/settings/recordings, and an amber moment being caught in a small screen. Keep broad quiet areas for exact text. Do not draw UI labels, QR patterns, company logos or a real person's likeness. Add text/QR in final layout, not by guessing in the model.
