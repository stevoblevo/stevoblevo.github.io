Before your first OBS recording, separate three places: the app, the settings, and the clips. Download OBS Studio from the official project. Use the normal Windows installer unless you deliberately need Portable mode. The app folder is not your recording folder. Open Settings, then Output, and choose where your clips should land. Run the Auto-Configuration Wizard for recording. Add one scene and capture only the game or window you intend to show. Check your voice and app sound, then close private windows and quiet notifications. Record one private minute in MKV. Stop, open the actual file, and watch it back. Picture, voice, app sound, privacy. Need an MP4? Use File, Remux Recordings. First prove the saved clip. Then tell the story.
