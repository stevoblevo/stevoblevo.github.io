Two patrons, three threads, one loch.

The Reign keeps the lanterns lit. The Hush puts them out. Their paper troopers meet on three threads every 24 seconds and cancel each other exactly. That is called a livelock, and it has been going on for some time. You are the part that isn't symmetrical.

Souls. Troopers drop a soul when they die. Half is yours on the spot; the other half floats up as an orb. Shoot your own orbs to secure them. Shoot theirs to deny them.

Structures. Each thread has a guardian, then a warden. They shrug off most damage unless your troopers are there. Drop one warden and the patron opens.

The loch. Three minutes in, the Ness surfaces between the Middle and East threads. It spits, it slams, and whoever lands the last hit gives their whole side the Greatness for 90 seconds.

The Hush. It has no heroes. It has hollows that walk the threads, and small ones that take what is lit and run for the gate. Every five minutes the rain comes down red.

Paper. The valley is paper. Paper folds.

Threads. Stand on a thread on your side and ride it to the front. It reaches further as you push.
