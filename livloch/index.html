Livloch

Three threads. One stalemate. Keep the little light.

The Reign keeps the lanterns lit. The Hush puts them out. Their paper troopers meet on three threads every 24 seconds and cancel each other exactly. That is called a livelock, and it has been going on for some time. You are the part that isn't symmetrical.

Souls. Troopers drop a soul when they die. Half is yours on the spot; the other half floats up as an orb. Shoot your own orbs to secure them. Shoot theirs to deny them.

Structures. Each thread has a guardian, then a warden. They shrug off most damage unless your troopers are there. Drop one warden and the patron opens.

The loch. Three minutes in, the Ness surfaces between the Middle and East threads. It spits, it slams, and whoever lands the last hit gives their whole side the Greatness for 90 seconds.

The Hush. It has no heroes. It has hollows that walk the threads, and small ones that take what is lit and run for the gate. Every five minutes the rain comes down red.

Views. You can change how you look at the valley. Some things are only there in one light. Before the red rain the valley has a mood, and one view answers it. Two views cost something to wear.

First rain. The short one. Six minutes: five to get ready, one of red rain, then five stars are counted. Start here.

Ever Fallen. The valley after the Hush has won. Nothing of theirs can be hurt. Your houses are dark. Every small one you put down gives a light back for a while. You will fall; the clock says when.

Lean back. Your hero plays itself. You keep three things: the view, which thread to stand on, and the fold. One thumb is enough.

Echo. The valley keeps your last few seconds. Ask for them back and you fight beside yourself for a moment.

Dreamwalk. Sit back and be shown: the valley plays itself and names each hidden thing as it happens. At the end it asks what you would keep.

Fruit. Things grow off the threads: lemons, rose hips, Verde Limón. Some are worth the walk. One is big.

Paper. The valley is paper. Paper folds. Its people are painted now, and so are its houses.

Threads. Stand on a thread on your side and ride it to the front. It reaches further as you push.
