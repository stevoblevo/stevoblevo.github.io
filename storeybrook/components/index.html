Components

Every component is plain markup with sb- classes, a few with a behavior from the bundle. Open one to see it live in the current look, with its rules.

Actions

ButtonPill-shaped actions in the interface face; one primary per view, in `accent`.SealA wax seal in `accent` for the one press that begins or keeps something: begin the book, sign it, keep this.

Inputs

ChoiceA row of pill-shaped radio choices for picking one of a few: who reads, the pace, how hard.LooksThe look picker: one button per look, each showing that look's paper, two of its pigments and its display face.SliderA range on a copper thread with an `accent` bead, for one continuous setting: reading speed, space, volume.ToggleAn on/off switch: a folded-paper tab sliding in a track.

Paper

PageOne book page: `page` with long fibres, a deckled edge and `shadow-page`, `size-page` wide, with a folio.SheetA sheet of `page` laid on the paper: a deckled edge, the paper's grain, `shadow-page`, held down with washi tape at up to two corners.SpreadTwo pages open like a book, with a gutter shadow down the middle; pages turn with a leaf.WashesWatercolor fields that bleed, mix and granulate: the only way large color arrives on the page.

Story

BalloonSpeech balloons for characters: a rounded balloon with an inked tail, a thought cloud, and a Comix shout.CaptionThe narrator's box over a picture: a slip of `surface` paper set a little askew, in the story face, with an optional speaker.PanelsComic panels: a grid of inked frames with captions, halftone and sound effects.ProseStory text and its marks: the lede, the body at the reading measure, a drop cap, ink links and the owner's handwritten note.ReaderThe read-aloud bar: a play button, the chapter's title, the voice in use and a meter, bound to the text it reads.

Play

BrookThe map of a game: a meandering watercolor brook with stepping stones that are scenes, chapters or mini-games.CreatureSaelion: a bunny, a tree octopus and a butterfly pixie at once, which grows from an egg.

Rewards

JarThe nougat jar: the count of every nougat a person has earned, filling toward a cap.LockA striped cover over something that is not open yet.StickerStickers for things found and places opened, kept in an album.

Shelf

ChipA small tag with a dot and a word, in one of five categorical colors: what kind of game, how long, what it needs.ShelfThe 99¢ Shelf: game covers standing on a copper board, scrolling sideways, each with a price tag on a string.

Navigation

TabsChapter tabs on the edge of the book: the open one is a `page` tab with an `accent` bar, pulled out from the stack.

Overlays

DialogA sheet that asks one question over a veiled page: a title in the display face, a sentence or two in the story face, and two buttons.DrawerA side sheet from the right for a book's contents, settings or the album.ToastA short message on a slip of sun-washed paper at the top of the screen: a reward, an unlock, a hint.

Transitions

BlotAn ink blot opens from the middle of an element to reveal it: for a new look, a found thing, the first picture of a chapter.FoldA view folds away like paper to show the next one underneath: the top half folds down over the bottom, then the folded sheet drops away.

Storeybrook 1.1.0. Shipped first in AND SO 7 and the 99¢ Shelf. Its dark counterpart is Depthfield.
