Prose

Story text and its marks: the lede, the body at the reading measure, a drop cap, ink links and the owner's handwritten note.

Provide - <p class="sb-lede"> for the first paragraph of a page (story-lede, up to 34em). - <div class="sb-prose"> around the story's paragraphs (story-body, fluid from 1.125rem to 1.375rem, at size-measure). - <span class="sb-dropcap">O</span> as the first letter of a chapter: the display face in accent, 4.6em, over a small sun wash (a square in Ink Blot, a gamboge panel in Comix). - <a class="sb-ink-link"> (or any link inside sb-prose): link with a wavy underline that darkens on hover; solid and 2px in Ink Blot. - <p class="sb-note"> for a handwritten note in cue (Caveat, 1.6rem).

At the kid density, ledes and prose switch to Andika at 1.3–1.6rem.

Do / Don't - Do use one drop cap per chapter and one note per view. - Don't justify, don't set story text in capitals, don't underline anything that is not a link.
