Shelf

The 99¢ Shelf: game covers standing on a copper board, scrolling sideways, each with a price tag on a string.

Provide <div class="sb-shelf"> holding covers. A cover is <a class="sb-cover" href="…"> with: - <span class="sb-price__string" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="sb-price"><span class="sb-price__amount">99¢</span><span class="sb-price__each">each</span></span> (sb-price--free turns the amount sap), - <span class="sb-cover__art"> (4:3, on surface: washes, the creature, or the game's own art as an image or canvas), - <span class="sb-cover__body"> with sb-cover__title (display face), sb-cover__line (one sentence) and sb-cover__meta (two or three Chips).

Options: sb-cover--new hangs a sap "New" ribbon; --tilt turns a cover a degree or two.

  • Covers are page with a spine shadow on the left edge, shadow-page, lifting 6px with shadow-lift on hover and focus; at the TV density the focused cover scales up 7% instead.
  • The shelf scrolls sideways with snap points (grid-auto-columns from 200px to size-cover) and sits on a copper board.

Do / Don't - Do write the price honestly; until a shop exists the page also says "Free to play now". - Do mark only one cover as new at a time. - Don't wrap the shelf into a grid of identical cards, and never put a countdown or a crossed-out price on a tag.

Storeybrook 1.0.0. Shipped first in AND SO 7 and the 99¢ Shelf. Its dark counterpart is Depthfield.
