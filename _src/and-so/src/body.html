GAMUTtunings & field guide

mood
light: consonance first; voices glide from one sweet spot to the next and a soft bed holds the root. lean: dissonances hang, then resolve. frolic: everything skips home on the beat. normie: the piano grid and familiar chords.
the reel
fourteen shots: every look, every view of the sound box, the 31-step floor, the weather, a crack in the obsidian. about two minutes. touch anything to take over.
motion
tilt rolls the voices and leans the sky. shake for a new chord, twist your wrist to turn the root, flick to strum.
the bubble on the top bar is a spirit level: tap it to set level, hold it to switch tilt off. a game controller works too: left stick moves a voice, A glides, B moves the root, X adds a voice, Y changes the view, the bumpers pick a voice, the triggers zoom.
listen
the demo hums first, so you can hear what listening does. the microphone stays off until you press it; pitch is worked out on this page and nothing is recorded or sent. headphones help it hear only you.
sound box
a Chladni plate, worked out from the physics: the chord shakes a metal plate from a stud at its centre, the sand is kicked off wherever the plate moves and comes to rest on the lines that stay still. higher notes, finer figures. tap the plate to sprinkle more; scroll over it to drive it harder.
tap the chip above the sound box, or press X, to change what it shows.
rounds
cover every target peg with a voice at rest. under 12 seconds earns three stars. every third round is by ear.
