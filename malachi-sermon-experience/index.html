Editing and caption uncertainties

The full recording is 38:07.272; this cut is 4:41.62. Captions are machine transcribed and locally reviewed, not verified word for word by Malachi. “In line of that” may be “in light of that”; a transition near the original 34:33 remains unclear. The gospel passage retains Christ’s voluntary death, God’s wrath in our place and resurrection. The closing retains the caveat that suffering is not always personal folly.

Art and toolkit provenance

The opening and closing use Steven’s supplied Aesthete illustration, unchanged. Intermediate scenery is original vector paper art. Storeybrook’s Origami surfaces, grain, fold transition and input semantics meet Depthfield’s Scene, ChapterMarker and SceneCaption components. Four-style controls preserve your place, reflection and listening position.
