This paper car and drive are a creative adaptation. Malachi describes a hailstorm and his car left in a parking lot; the sermon does not say he was driving through it.

The radio plays continuous passages from his original recording, edited and sometimes reordered. There is no synthetic voice. Captions are machine transcribed and locally reviewed, with uncertainty retained in the illustrated experience.

The storm cues follow the cut: hail begins at 0:03.72; stripped plants at 0:10.41; damaged windows at 0:12.95; hammered cars at 0:14.97; the parking-lot reflection at 0:26.23. The scene suggests weather, without flashes or simulated impacts on the viewer.

The paper driver borrows the flat cap, glasses, beard and plaid cues from Steven’s Aesthete reference. It is an original stylized mesh, not a photographic likeness.

Official sermon and notes ↗

Parked stops road motion. Quiet motion also stills rain, hail, wipers and camera easing. Rain sound starts muted and lowers automatically under speech. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
